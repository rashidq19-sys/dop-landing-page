Stop 01 — What changes

Scale has a price.
It's paid in management hours.

Most DSPs run on a rota spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group and a handful of separate tools. It works, until volume rises or you add a second station, and then it quietly eats your managers' days. Here's what stops.

Chasing availability on WhatsApp all weekend→Drivers set their own rota in the app
Ringing round at 06:30 to see who turned up→Check-in board fills itself as they arrive
Typing the plan into Amazon's system by hand→One tap publishes it to every driver's phone
A separate spreadsheet for every station→Every station under one login
Onboarding packs emailed back and forth→Drivers onboard themselves, you approve
Driver invoices rebuilt by hand each week→Generated, sent and exported in minutes

Same operation. Roughly 32 fewer admin hours a week, per site.

Stop 02 — What it does

Every daily job, one login.

A glimpse of each — tap any image to see it larger.

Dispatch & deployment

Smart Assign fills every route from live availability, you adjust, then one tap publishes waves, routes and vans to every driver's phone.

~4 hrs/week back

Rota & availability

Drivers mark themselves ON or OFF for every day in their own app. The week fills itself, locks when you need certainty, and shortfalls show up days early instead of at 06:00.

~9 hrs/week back

Driver performance

Every metric Amazon scores your drivers on, weekly, per driver: DCR, DNR DPMO, POD, contact compliance, CDF and customer escalations — with worst-offender lists per metric and a league table your drivers can see too.

Better network scores

Multi-station, one account

Running more than one station? Each is its own space inside the same account, with its own rota, deployment board, drivers and vans. Switch between them from the top of the screen — no separate logins, no separate spreadsheets, and the owner sees all of them.

One or twenty stations

Announcements & messaging

Send a notice to every driver, one station, or a single person — it lands as a push notification in their app, and you see who has actually read it. One-to-one chat is built in too, so operational messages stop living in personal WhatsApp.

Proof it was read

Van checks & damage AI

A guided 10-angle walkaround from the driver's phone, morning and evening. Every photo is timestamped and tied to that driver and that van, and AI flags new dents and scratches — so a damage charge is settled with evidence, not memory.

Disputes end here

Compliance & PCNs

Licences, right to work, passports, insurance, MOT and road tax tracked per driver and per van, with everything needing attention on one screen. Photograph a penalty notice and DSPOps reads it and finds the driver who had that van that day.

~2 hrs/week back

Generate driver invoices

Upload the week's report and DSPOps generates every driver's invoice — day rates, same-day rates, bonuses and deductions — then sends it to each driver in the app and gives you a clean export for your accountant.

~6 hrs/week back

Also included: same-day delivery waves · live route progress · incident & accident reports · fuel logs · repair and off-road tracking · shift history & reliability reporting · e-signed agreements · role-based access and 2FA.

Stop 03 — The three that matter most

Getting in, turning up, and moving over.

The parts that decide whether new software actually sticks with a driver workforce.

Check-in that answers 06:30

The driver taps "I'm here" at the depot. You get the exact arrival time, how many minutes late they are against their own start time, and how far from the station they were standing — so a driver who checks in from two miles away is flagged, not assumed.

The distance flag raises a question — it never blocks a driver from working.

Drivers onboard themselves

A new starter gets a link and completes eight guided steps on their phone — no packs emailed back and forth. You get a review queue: approve, reject or ask for a better photo. ID numbers are encrypted, and agreements are signed in the app.

1Personal details & address history
2Emergency contact
3ID documents & right to work
4Tax, payroll & medical declaration
5Agreement, signed on screen
Switch it off per company if you onboard your own way.

Moving over takes days

Send your driver and van lists as a spreadsheet — we give you the template, or just send what you already have and we do it. Your station is set up, drivers install the app, and you run tomorrow's dispatch in DSPOps. No IT project, no downtime.

Setup and data import are done for you.
