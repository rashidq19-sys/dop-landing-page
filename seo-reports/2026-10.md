## 3. Top 3 opportunities

### 1. Stop the repo outranking the product for its own name

For `dspops`, search shows our internal SEO reports on GitHub, not dspops.app. Anyone given the
name in a sales call who searches it lands on working notes — including competitor analysis.

- **Make `rashidq19-sys/dop-landing-page` private** (preferred), or at minimum set the repo's
  About → Website field to `https://dspops.app` so the GitHub result at least links to the product.
- Note: this report itself is committed to that same public repo, as the scheduled task requires.
- Then do the citations from last month that would give Google a real "DSPOps" entity:
  LinkedIn company page, Companies House name match, Crunchbase.

### 2. Get onto GetApp UK / Capterra UK / SoftwareAdvice UK

These directories appeared on **both** commercial UK queries this month (they surfaced once in
September). They are the single clearest route into the `amazon dsp management software uk`
results, which a site this young cannot win directly. Listing is free on the Gartner network
(GetApp, Capterra and SoftwareAdvice share one vendor profile). Pick the "Delivery Service Partner"
/ fleet category, not "Amazon DSP" (which is classified as ad-tech there).

### 3. Answer DSPilot with a comparison page and the van-inspection niche

DSPilot is a UK operator selling almost the same product with a free trial and public pricing.
Two moves:

- A **"DSPOps vs DSPilot / Zerity / Courio"** comparison page — buyers searching competitor names
  are the highest-intent traffic available, and none of the three has one.
- Keep pressing `/van-inspection-app`. The SERP now includes one UK tool (Quartix Check) so it is
  slightly less open than in September, but **no result is Amazon-DSP-specific**. Internal links
  from the van-damage and inspection-compliance blog posts, plus DVSA daily-walkaround content,
  remain the cheapest route.
